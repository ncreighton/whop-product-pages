Frequently Asked Questions

How are the STL files tested before they’re sent?

Every model is printed on multiple common printers (Prusa, Bambu Lab, Creality, etc.) to confirm dimensional accuracy, layer adhesion, and ease of support removal. We only ship designs that survive our full print test.

What file format and slicer profiles do you provide?

All files are delivered as STL format. We also include suggested slicer settings (layer height, infill, temperature) for popular profiles like PrusaSlicer and Cura, so you can get started immediately.

Are the models suitable for both FDM and resin printers?

Yes. We clearly label each drop with recommended technology (FDM, SLA, or both). Most models are designed to work on either, but we always include guidance for supports and orientation based on your setup.

What kind of designs can I expect each month?

Each monthly drop blends functional prints (tools, organizers, machine upgrades) with artistic and decorative models. We also include modular or multi-part builds for those who enjoy assembly and painting.

Can I modify or remix the STL files I receive?

Absolutely. Your subscription grants a personal maker license — you can modify, remix, and print as many copies as you like for personal use. We love seeing what the community creates from our base designs.
