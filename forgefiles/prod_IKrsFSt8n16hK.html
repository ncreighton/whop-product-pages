Frequently Asked Questions

Are these files compatible with both resin and FDM printers?

Yes. Each STL is optimized for both technologies. For resin printers, we include pre-supported versions with light and heavy supports. For FDM, the models feature no overhangs beyond 45° and include flat bases for stable bed adhesion.

Do I need to repair the meshes before slicing?

No. All files are watertight with no flipped normals, open edges, or degenerate faces. They’re ready to drag-and-drop into your slicer (PrusaSlicer, Cura, Lychee, Chitubox, etc.) without any repair step.

What print settings do you recommend for the best detail?

For resin, we suggest a 0.05mm layer height with 2-second exposure (standard resin). For FDM, a 0.12mm layer height and 205°C nozzle (PLA) gives sharp detail. Each model includes a recommended settings cheat sheet inside the pack.

Can I scale the models beyond the included variants?

Absolutely—but scaling up too far may reveal minor surface detail limits. The included variants are optimized for detail retention at each size. If you need a specific scale for a multi-part build, you can safely scale within ±20% of the included sizes.

Do you offer commercial licenses or refunds if a file fails to print?

Yes, a commercial license is available as an add-on. As for failed prints: if a file has a geometry error that causes failure, we'll replace it immediately. However, we cannot refund for issues caused by printer calibration or material settings—our support team will help you dial those in.
