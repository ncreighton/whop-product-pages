Frequently Asked Questions

Which protocols does the database cover?

We cover Zigbee 3.0, Z-Wave (500/700/800 series, including Z-Wave Long Range), Thread, Matter 1.0/1.2, Bluetooth 4.x–5.x, Wi-Fi 4/5/6, and dozens of proprietary RF and wired protocols (Insteon, Lutron Clear Connect, Caséta, Hue, Aqara, etc.). Every listing specifies exact revision and frequency band.

How do you handle firmware or revision changes that affect compatibility?

We maintain a live changelog for each device entry. If a manufacturer releases a firmware update that adds or drops protocol support—say, a hub gaining Thread after a software update—the database reflects that within 48 hours of verified testing.

Can I use this to plan a new smart home build from scratch?

Absolutely. You can filter by network topology: choose your primary hub (e.g., Hubitat, Home Assistant, SmartThings, Apple Home, Google Home, Alexa), and the database shows only peripherals proven to work with that controller. No ‘should work’ guesswork.

Is this just for consumer smart home gear, or does it include professional/commercial equipment?

Both. We cover residential devices (lighting, locks, sensors, thermostats, shades) and commercial-grade controllers (Lutron Vive, Control4, Crestron, Leviton) used in multi-room or business installs. Each entry is tagged for deployment tier.

How often is the data updated, and what's your source?

Every entry is validated through hands-on testing by our team of smart home engineers. We do not scrape manufacturer specs or user forums. Updates are pushed weekly, with urgent revisions (e.g., a compatibility-breaking firmware patch) published within 24 hours.
