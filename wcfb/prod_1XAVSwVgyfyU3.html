Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any special tools or ingredients to start?

Not at all. We begin with what you already have—your breath, your focus, and a bit of curiosity. Candles, herbs, and crystals can come later, but your own presence is the most powerful tool you'll ever hold.

What if I've tried before and nothing 'worked'?

That happens more often than you'd think, and it's not a sign of failure. Sometimes we're reaching before we're truly listening. This course helps you settle into the rhythm of intention and patience, so your magic has room to breathe.

Is this about Wicca, or is it for any path?

This is for anyone who feels a pull toward the unseen—whether you're drawn to kitchen witchery, folk magic, or simply want to honor the cycles of the moon. No specific tradition required, just an open heart.

How long do I have access to the materials?

Once you're in, it's yours to revisit whenever you need. Magic doesn't rush, and neither do we. You can move through the lessons at your own pace, returning to the grounding practices and spells as often as you like.

I'm nervous I'll do something wrong. What if I make a mistake?

Mistakes are part of any living practice—they're how we learn the taste of our own energy. I'll guide you gently, and remind you that magic is a conversation, not a test. You're allowed to stumble, to laugh, and to try again.
