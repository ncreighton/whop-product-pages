Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience to use this kit?

Not at all. This kit was made especially for those who are just starting to explore. Everything is written clearly and gently, like a friend showing you their favorite corner of the garden.

Is this kit tied to a specific tradition or religion?

No. The practices here are inspired by nature-based and seasonal witchcraft, but they're open to all paths. You're welcome to adapt them in whatever way feels true to you.

What exactly is included in the box?

Inside you'll find: a handwritten-style grimoire with 7 seasonal rituals, three small stones (clear quartz, rose quartz, and amethyst), and a short guide to getting started with your first ritual. Each piece is chosen to feel supportive, not overwhelming.

Can I practice these rituals if I live in an apartment or city?

Yes, absolutely. The rituals are designed to be done in small, everyday spaces — a windowsill, a nightstand, even a quiet corner of your kitchen. You don't need a forest or a field; just a little stillness.

I'm worried about doing something 'wrong.' Is there a right way?

The only 'wrong' way is to force yourself into something that doesn't feel right. Magic is deeply personal, and this kit is an invitation to explore, not a test. Trust your intuition, and let the rituals be a gentle guide, not a rulebook.
