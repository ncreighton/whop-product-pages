Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any special tools or ingredients to start?

Not at all. The only real tool you need is your own intention. We'll teach you how to work with what you already have — a candle from the kitchen, a stone from the garden, or simply your own heartbeat.

I’ve tried other witchcraft books and felt overwhelmed. How is this different?

This is not a thick encyclopedia of rules. Think of it as a soft hand reaching out in the dark. We keep things simple, heart-centered, and focused on your personal experience, so you can breathe and grow at your own pace.

Is this for people who practice alone, without a coven?

Yes, absolutely. This guide is written especially for the solitary seeker — the one who feels the call in the quiet moments. You'll find plenty of support for crafting a beautiful, powerful practice all on your own.

I’m not sure if I even have any intuition. Can this still help me?

Everyone has intuition — it's the quiet voice you've been ignoring. We'll help you gently untangle it from all the noise, with simple exercises that feel like playing, not performing. Trust grows slowly, and we'll walk that path together.

Will this teach me to cast spells and work with the moon?

Yes, and more than that. You'll learn to craft spells that feel true to you, honor the cycles of the moon, and weave intention into your everyday life. But always from a place of warmth and self-compassion, not pressure.
