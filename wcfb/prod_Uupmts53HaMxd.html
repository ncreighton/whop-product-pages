Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any special tools or supplies to start?

Not at all. Your greatest tool is your own intention. We’ll show you how to work with things you already have—a candle, a bowl of water, a handful of salt—before you ever think about buying anything fancy.

I’ve never done any kind of magic before. Will I be lost?

This path is made for exactly where you are. Think of me as a friend walking beside you, pointing out the mossy stones and quiet clearings. We begin with the simplest practices—like noticing the moon’s phase and setting a daily intention.

Is this rooted in a specific tradition, or can I blend it with my own beliefs?

This is a gentle, open-handed approach that honors many paths. You are welcome to blend it with your own spirituality or keep it purely personal. The only rule is that it feels true to you.

How much time do I need to dedicate each week?

As much or as little as you like. Some lessons take five minutes; others invite you to sit with the moon for a quiet evening. The practice bends to your schedule, not the other way around.

What if I feel silly or unsure about doing magic?

That’s a sign you’re being honest—and that’s a beautiful place to start. We’ll move at a pace that lets you feel the magic before you have to name it. Soon, that awkwardness softens into wonder, like learning to hum a tune you already know.
