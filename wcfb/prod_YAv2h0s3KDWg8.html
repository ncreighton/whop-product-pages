Frequently Asked Questions

I'm brand new to witchcraft—will this feel overwhelming?

Not at all. This guide is written as if we're sitting together over tea, exploring the craft at your own pace. Every ritual includes clear steps and a warm explanation, so you'll never feel lost or pressured.

Do I need special tools or a physical altar to start?

Just your presence and intention. A quiet corner, a candle, and the herbs mentioned are lovely, but we'll also share simple substitutions using items you likely already have at home.

How does the automation actually work?

Think of it as a gentle nudge from the moon herself. You'll receive personalized reminders and prompts aligned with the lunar cycle—like when to gather your herbs for a new moon ritual or when to sit in stillness under the full moon.

Can I use these rituals in a group or coven, or are they solo only?

Both! The rituals are designed for one or many. If you're building a coven, you'll find simple ways to adapt each practice for a circle—sharing intentions, passing the herbs, or casting a group protection blessing.

What if I miss a moon phase or fall out of rhythm?

The craft is forgiving, and so are we. Every ritual includes a 'catch-up' variation, and the automation lets you pause or restart anytime. The moon will always welcome you back.
