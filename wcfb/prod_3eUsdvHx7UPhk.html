Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to already know anything about witchcraft to use this?

Not a single thing. This book is for the person who has felt a pull toward the moon but wasn't sure where to start. We begin exactly where you are — no prior experience, no fancy tools, no pressure.

What if I don't have access to special herbs, crystals, or tools?

Then you're ready. Every spell in here uses ingredients you likely already have in your kitchen or home — sugar, salt, cinnamon, a white candle, tap water. The magic lives in your intention, not in the objects.

How is this different from other witchcraft books I've seen?

Most books hand you a list of rules and rituals to follow. This one holds your hand and says, 'Look — you’re already doing it.' It focuses on the quiet, everyday magic you might have overlooked, and gently shows you how to honor and deepen those instincts.

Will I learn about gods, goddesses, or specific traditions?

Only if you’re curious to. This book stays focused on natural, intuitive practice and doesn’t require you to work with any specific deity or tradition. You’re free to explore and weave in your own beliefs as you feel called.

Can I practice these spells if I live with others or have very little private space?

Absolutely. The rituals are designed to be small, quiet, and subtle — a whispered intention over your morning tea, a pinch of salt before you leave the house, a candle lit for a few minutes at your desk. Your practice can be as visible or invisible as you need it to be.
