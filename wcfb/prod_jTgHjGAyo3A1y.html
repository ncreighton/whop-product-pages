Frequently Asked Questions

I’m totally new to witchcraft. Where do I even start?

Right here, dear one. This guide was written with the absolute beginner in mind—no prior knowledge needed. You’ll start with a warm, simple exercise that helps you feel your own intuition before you ever touch a tool or a spell.

Do I need to buy a lot of special tools or ingredients?

Not at all. This book focuses on what you already have—a candle, a spoon, a quiet corner. Magick lives in the ordinary, and we’ll explore how to see it there.

What if I don’t feel 'witchy' enough?

If you’re curious, you’re already witchy enough. There’s no test to pass, no threshold to cross. This practice is about welcoming your natural instincts, not proving anything to anyone.

Is this book connected to any specific tradition or religion?

It’s designed to be a gentle, open door. You won’t find dogma here—just soft frameworks and invitations. You can weave this into any spiritual path or keep it as a standalone, personal practice.

How is this different from other witchcraft books for beginners?

Most books hand you a list of spells and correspondences. This one hands you a mirror. It helps you discover your own rhythms and symbols first, so the magick you create feels like a true expression of you—not a copy of someone else’s.
