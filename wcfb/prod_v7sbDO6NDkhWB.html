Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any special tools or supplies to start?

Not at all. Your most powerful tool is already with you — your intention. A simple candle, a cup of water, or even a smooth stone from your garden can become a focus for your magic. As you go, you’ll discover what feels right for you.

Is witchcraft a religion? Do I have to follow certain beliefs?

Witchcraft is a practice, not a religion. You can absolutely be a witch and also follow any faith or none at all. This path is about connecting with nature, yourself, and the energy around you — your personal beliefs are always honored here.

I’m worried about doing something ‘wrong.’ What if I mess up a spell?

There’s no such thing as a ‘wrong’ feeling in witchcraft — only learning. Think of it like baking with a friend: if you add a little too much cinnamon, it’s still delicious, and next time you’ll know just how much you prefer. Magic is the same. Your intention is the heart of it, and mistakes are just part of the journey.

How much time do I need to dedicate each day or week?

As much as feels good for you. Some days you might spend five minutes grounding before bed; other days you might explore a full ritual. The course is designed to fit into real life, not take it over. Even a single deep breath with intention is a beautiful act of magic.

Will this teach me to cast spells and work with the moon phases?

Yes, gently and step by step. You’ll start with simple intention-setting and gradually learn to align with the moon’s phases, create small spells for clarity or protection, and build your own rituals. Every lesson builds on the last, so you always feel steady and confident.
