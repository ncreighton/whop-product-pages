Frequently Asked Questions

I've never practiced witchcraft before — is this book really for me?

Yes, absolutely. This guide was written for the curious beginner, the one who feels a pull but doesn't know where to start. Think of it as a friend sitting beside you with a cup of tea, showing you how to notice the moon without needing to memorize a single phase.

Do I need special tools or ingredients to start?

Not at all. Everything you need is probably already in your kitchen or garden. We focus on what's around you — salt, water, a candle, a sprig of mint — so you can practice magic without a shopping list.

Is this about Wicca, or does it cover other traditions?

This book draws from folk magic, kitchen witchery, and personal intuition rather than a single tradition. You're free to weave in your own heritage, beliefs, or simply let your inner knowing be your guide — no dogma, just discovery.

What if I don't feel 'magical' yet? Can I still learn?

That quiet feeling of not being magical enough? That's exactly where your magic begins. We start with small, mundane moments — stirring your tea with intention, thanking a tree — until you realize you've been practicing all along.

How is this different from other beginner witchcraft books?

Most books hand you a list of rules. This one hands you a lantern and walks beside you. There are no wrong answers here — only your own growing sense of wonder, and rituals that bend to fit your real, messy, beautiful life.
