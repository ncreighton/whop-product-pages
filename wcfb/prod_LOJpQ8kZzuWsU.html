Frequently Asked Questions

I don't have any crystals yet. Can I still use this guide?

Absolutely. This guide was written for curious beginners, and it includes beautiful suggestions for your first few stones — along with how to find them without overwhelm. No collection required, just a willing heart.

Do I need to be 'psychic' or already spiritually gifted?

Not at all. Every single person already has an inner sense — call it intuition, a gut feeling, or a quiet knowing. This guide simply helps you recognize and trust that voice when you hold a crystal. It's like learning to listen to a soft, friendly whisper.

Is crystal correspondence just about memorizing meanings?

Oh, not at all. True correspondence is a dance between you and the stone. While we'll touch on traditional associations, the real magic is in feeling what a specific crystal wants to share with you, right here and now. It's a living conversation, not a list.

Can I use these teachings for spellwork or rituals?

Yes, and in the coziest way. The guide gently shows you how to invite crystals into simple rituals — like setting an intention for your day or creating a tiny altar for restful sleep. It's about weaving magic into moments that already feel like yours.

What if I pick up a crystal and feel nothing at all?

Then you are in exactly the right place. That quiet, blank feeling is just as meaningful as a tingle or a rush of warmth. We'll explore why that happens and how to gently invite a sense of connection — no pressure, no rushing. Your intuition unfolds in its own time.
