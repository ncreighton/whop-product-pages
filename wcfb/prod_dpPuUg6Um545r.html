Frequently Asked Questions

I've never done a spell before — will I feel silly?

Not for a moment. This guide starts with a grounding ritual that feels more like a deep breath than a performance. You'll ease into spellwork like you're learning a new lullaby — at your own pace, with warmth and patience.

Do I need special tools like crystals or herbs?

Nope. All you need is a candle, your breath, and a whisper of intention. This book is for the person who wants to try witchcraft without a shopping list. Fancy tools can come later — or not at all. Your magic is enough.

How long does each spell take?

Each of the three moon spells takes ten minutes or less. The grounding ritual is even quicker — it's meant to be a pause, not a project. Perfect for a quiet moment before bed or a stolen minute in the middle of the day.

What if I don't feel anything during the spell?

That's completely normal, and even beautiful. Magic doesn't always announce itself with shivers or light shows. Sometimes it's just a quiet shift — a thought that softens, a tension that releases. Trust the process, and let your intuition catch up in its own time.

Is this book for someone who follows a specific path, like Wicca?

Not at all. This is for anyone who feels a pull toward the moon and wonders if there's more beneath the surface. You don't need to belong to any tradition — just bring your open heart and your curiosity. That's more than enough.
